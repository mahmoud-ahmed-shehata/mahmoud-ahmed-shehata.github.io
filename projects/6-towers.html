Challenge

The situation on site

International construction delivery under cross-cultural conditions — managing site execution, quality control and daily coordination with local subcontractors and a local team in a market with different working practices, standards and communication dynamics. The challenge was maintaining consistent quality and execution discipline across RC structural works, blockwork and finishing while adapting to local team structures and ensuring that surveying accuracy and QA/QC standards remained in line with project specifications regardless of the local context.
Role & Actions

What I specifically did

01 Supervised daily structural works — foundations, columns, slabs and staircases across all 6 residential towers — ensuring execution followed approved drawings and structural requirements.
02 Carried out surveying and setting-out for grids, levels and key structural elements — ensuring execution accuracy before works commenced on each structural activity.
03 Monitored blockwork, plaster and basic finishing activities — checking that all works followed drawings, quality requirements and specifications from the structural phase through to finishes.
04 Coordinated with the project engineer and local foremen to sequence activities and avoid delays on critical structural items — keeping the programme moving across all towers simultaneously.
05 Checked reinforcement installation, formwork and concrete pours — raising any discrepancies to the senior engineer and ensuring that no structural work was poured without proper inspection.
06 Followed up with local subcontractors on manpower, work fronts and material deliveries — resolving technical issues on site while maintaining productive relationships with cross-cultural teams.
07 Prepared site reports recording daily progress, highlighting constraints and material shortages — providing the project team with a clear and accurate picture of site status across all towers.
08 Coordinated with local consultants or representatives for inspections and clarifications — navigating the approval process in an unfamiliar market while keeping works moving forward.
