Baniyas Car Showroom

I led site execution for a commercial showroom in Rahayl Cars City, working alongside the Project Manager from piling completion through handover.

Role
Project Engineer
Project value
Approx. AED 50 million
Daily workforce
80 to 150
Solar installation
1,092 panels

My role

  • Coordinated reinforced concrete and structural steel works, aluminium cladding, standing-seam panels, skylights and custom glazing.
  • Coordinated ventilation, smoke extraction and fire protection works. Supervised external drainage across yards and parking areas.
  • Coordinated installation of the solar shading system and sequenced subcontractor work around inspections and handover.
  • Reviewed shop drawings and managed RFIs, work and material inspections, sample approvals and quality records with the consultant.

Handover

Sequenced the works to support a partial handover while remaining work continued. Closed 32 non-conformance reports, with none outstanding at final handover.
