About

I’m a Civil Project Engineer based in Abu Dhabi. My site experience covers residential towers, private villas and commercial showrooms, with work spanning reinforced concrete, structural steel, facades and high-end finishes.

I turn the project programme into short-term look-ahead plans. Before work starts, I check drawing and material readiness, raise technical queries and coordinate the sequence with subcontractors and the consultant.

During execution, I track daily progress, arrange inspections and follow up on quality issues. As areas approach handover, I coordinate snag closure and prepare the records the project team needs for closeout.

B.Sc. in Civil Engineering, New Cairo Academy, 2019.
